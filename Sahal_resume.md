## Selected Publications

### BiMediX2: Bio-Medical EXpert LMM for Diverse Medical Modalities
**Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**\*, Mohammed Irfan Kurpath\*, Sara Pieri, Saeed Yahya Alseiari, Shanavas Cholakkal, Khaled Aldahmani, Fahad Khan, Rao Anwer, Salman Khan, Timothy Baldwin, Hisham Cholakkal  
**Venue:** EMNLP 2025 – Findings  
**Notes:** Meta Llama Impact Award, LinkedIn Feature  
**Description:** BiMediX2 is a bilingual Arabic-English medical multimodal model that unifies text and image understanding for diverse clinical tasks, including multi-turn conversation, VQA, report generation, and summarization. It is trained on a 1.6M-sample bilingual healthcare dataset and achieves strong state-of-the-art results across both medical language and multimodal benchmarks.

### GLaMM: Pixel Grounding Large Multimodal Model
Hanoona Rasheed\*, Muhammad Maaz\*, **Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**, Abdelrahman Shaker, Salman Khan, Hisham Cholakkal, Rao M Anwer, Eric Xing, Ming-Hsuan Yang, Fahad S Khan  
**Venue:** CVPR 2024 (arXiv 2023)  
**Notes:** HuggingFace Featured, GitHub  
**Description:** GLaMM introduces a multimodal model that generates natural language responses grounded with pixel-level segmentation masks, enabling far more precise visual grounding than standard multimodal chat models. The paper also introduces a large-scale grounding dataset and evaluation setup for grounded conversation, while showing strong performance on segmentation, captioning, and conversational grounding tasks.  
**Links:**
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03356
- Hugging Face paper page: https://huggingface.co/papers/2311.03356
- GitHub: https://github.com/mbzuai-oryx/groundingLMM

### MediX-R1: Open Ended Medical Reinforcement Learning
**Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**\*, Mohammed Irfan Kurpath\*, Omair Mohamed, Mohamed Zidan, Fahad Khan, Salman Khan, Rao Anwer, Hisham Cholakkal  
**Venue:** arXiv 2026  
**Description:** MediX-R1 proposes an open-ended reinforcement learning framework for medical multimodal models, moving beyond brittle multiple-choice training toward clinically grounded free-form answers. Its composite reward design and LLM-as-judge evaluation framework improve open-ended medical reasoning across both text-only and image-text benchmarks, despite using only about 51K instruction samples.  
**Links:**
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23363
- Project: https://medix.cvmbzuai.com/
- GitHub: https://github.com/mbzuai-oryx/MediX-R1

### BiMediX: Bilingual Medical Mixture of Experts LLM
Sara Pieri\*, **Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**\*, Fahad Shahbaz Khan, Rao Muhammad Anwer, Salman Khan, Timothy Baldwin, Hisham Cholakkal  
**Venue:** EMNLP 2024 – Findings  
**Description:** BiMediX is the first bilingual Arabic-English medical mixture-of-experts LLM designed for open-ended QA, multi-turn medical dialogue, and multiple-choice reasoning in both languages. The work introduces a large bilingual medical instruction corpus and benchmark, showing that multilingual medical tuning can improve both accessibility and performance.

### MAviS: A Multimodal Conversational Assistant For Avian Species
Yevheniia Kryklyvets, Mohammed Irfan Kurpath, **Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**, Jinxing Zhou, Fahad Shahbaz Khan, Rao Muhammad Anwer, Salman Khan, Hisham Cholakkal  
**Venue:** EMNLP 2025  
**Notes:** SAC Highlight Award  
**Description:** MAviS develops a domain-specialized multimodal assistant for birds by combining image, audio, and text understanding for fine-grained species recognition and multimodal question answering. The paper also introduces MAviS-Dataset and MAviS-Bench, showing that domain-adaptive multimodal models can substantially improve biodiversity and ecological AI applications.  
**Links:**
- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1455/
- Award page: https://2025.emnlp.org/program/awards/
- GitHub: https://github.com/yevheniia-uv/MAviS

### LLMVoX: Autoregressive Streaming Text-to-Speech Model for Any LLM
Sambal Shikhar, Mohammed Irfan Kurpath, **Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**, Jean Lahoud, Fahad Shahbaz Khan, Rao Muhammad Anwer, Salman Khan, Hisham Cholakkal  
**Venue:** ACL 2025 – Findings  
**Description:** LLMVoX is a lightweight streaming text-to-speech model that can plug into any LLM without modifying the underlying language model, preserving its reasoning and conversational quality. It achieves low-latency speech generation, supports long-form dialogue, generalizes across backbones, and extends naturally to multilingual and multimodal settings.  
**Links:**
- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1051/
- GitHub: https://github.com/mbzuai-oryx/LLMVoX

### XrayGPT: Chest Radiographs Summarization using Large Medical Vision-Language Models
Omkar Chakradhar Thawakar\*, Abdelrahman M Shaker\*, **Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**\*, Hisham Cholakkal, Rao Muhammad Anwer, Salman Khan, Jorma Laaksonen, Fahad Khan  
**Venue:** ACL 2024 – BioNLP  
**Description:** XrayGPT aligns a medical visual encoder with a language model to enable open-ended conversational understanding of chest X-rays rather than only fixed report generation. The paper shows that high-quality summary-style instruction data from radiology reports can support interactive radiograph understanding and clinically meaningful follow-up question answering.  
**Links:**
- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2024.bionlp-1.35/
- GitHub: https://github.com/mbzuai-oryx/XrayGPT

### Arabic Mini-ClimateGPT: A Climate Change and Sustainability Tailored Arabic LLM
**Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**\*, Abdelrahman Shaker\*, Omkar Thawakar\*, Hisham Cholakkal, Rao Muhammad Anwer, Salman Khan, Fahad Khan  
**Venue:** EMNLP 2023 – Findings; NeurIPS 2023 – ENLSP  
**Description:** Arabic Mini-ClimateGPT is a lightweight Arabic LLM specialized for climate change and sustainability, built using a large conversational instruction dataset and retrieval-based support during inference. It improves the quality of Arabic climate-related responses over baseline open models and demonstrates the value of domain-specific LLMs for socially important topics.  
**Links:**
- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.941/
- NeurIPS page: https://neurips.cc/virtual/2023/81199
- GitHub: https://github.com/mbzuai-oryx/ClimateGPT

### A Benchmark and Agentic Framework for Omni-Modal Reasoning and Tool Use in Long Videos
Mohammed Irfan Kurpath\*, Jaseel Muhammad Kaithakkodan\*, Jinxing Zhou, **Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**, Mohammad Almansoori, Noor Ahsan, Beknur Kalmakhanbet, Sambal Shikhar, Rishabh Lalla, Jean Lahoud, Mariette Awad, Fahad Shahbaz Khan, Salman Khan, Rao Muhammad Anwer, Hisham Cholakkal  
**Venue:** CVPR 2026 – Findings  
**Description:** This work introduces LongShOT, a benchmark and agentic framework for long-video understanding that goes beyond vision-only tasks by incorporating speech, audio, tool use, and open-ended reasoning. It is designed to diagnose failures in perception, cross-modal integration, and long-context reasoning, making evaluation of real-world omni-modal video systems more realistic and interpretable.  
**Links:**
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16978
- GitHub: https://github.com/mbzuai-oryx/longshot

### Semi-supervised Open-World Object Detection
**Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**, Abhishek Singh Gehlot, Rao Muhammad Anwer, Fahad Shahbaz Khan, Hisham Cholakkal  
**Venue:** AAAI 2024 – Main Track  
**Description:** This paper introduces semi-supervised open-world object detection, a more realistic setting where new object categories are learned with limited labels and large amounts of unlabeled data rather than relying heavily on human annotation. It proposes SS-OWFormer, which improves feature alignment and pseudo-labeling for unknown objects, showing strong results across multiple datasets including remote sensing benchmarks.  
**Links:**
- Paper: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/28227
- GitHub: https://github.com/sahalshajim/SS-OWFormer

### MuCoT: Multilingual Contrastive Training for Question-Answering in Low-resource Languages
Gokul Karthik Kumar, Abhishek Gehlot, **Sahal Shaji Mullappilly**, Karthik Nandakumar  
**Venue:** ACL 2022 – DravidianLangTech Oral  
**Description:** MuCoT studies question answering for low-resource languages by augmenting training with translation and transliteration across languages, then applying multilingual contrastive learning during fine-tuning. The paper shows that contrastive training helps reduce degradation from cross-language transfer and improves QA performance in low-resource multilingual settings.  
**Links:**
- Paper: https://aclanthology.org/2022.dravidianlangtech-1.3/
- GitHub: https://github.com/gokulkarthik/mucot
